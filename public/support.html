Flash — Support

German flashcards with spaced repetition.

Flash is a spaced-repetition flashcard app built for learning German. You make decks, review cards on a schedule that adapts to how well you remember them, and can import existing Anki .apkg decks. It runs on iPhone and in any browser at flash.luquematte.com, with the same account on both.

Getting help

Email luquemar91@gmail.com. It is a one-person project, so replies are personal but not instant — expect a couple of days. Including your username and what you were doing when something went wrong makes it much easier to fix.

Common questions

How do I delete my account and all my data?

In the app, on the decks screen, scroll to the footer and tap Delete account. This immediately and permanently removes your account, decks, cards and review history. You do not need to email anyone to have it done.

The German text-to-speech sounds wrong, or does not speak at all.

Flash uses the German voices installed on your device. If none is installed, iOS falls back to an English voice or stays silent. Install one under Settings › Accessibility › Spoken Content › Voices › Deutsch, then reopen Flash. The higher-quality voices are a larger download but sound considerably better.

Can I import my existing Anki decks?

Yes — Flash imports Anki .apkg files, including media-free note types. Very large or unusually structured decks may not import cleanly; if one fails, email the deck name and roughly how many cards it has.

Do I need an internet connection?

Yes. Your decks and review scheduling live on the server so they stay in sync between your phone and the web, so studying requires a connection.

I forgot my password / cannot sign in.

Email luquemar91@gmail.com from the address on the account. If you originally signed in with Apple or Google, use that same button again rather than a password — signing in the other way creates a separate account.

Privacy

Flash has no analytics, no ads and no tracking. See the privacy policy for exactly what is stored and how to delete it.

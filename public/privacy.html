Privacy Policy

Flash — German Flashcards · Last updated 12 August 2026

Flash is a small, independently built flashcard app. It is not a business, and your study data is not a product. This policy describes exactly what the app stores and what it does not do.

What is stored

When you create an account and use the app, the following is stored:

  • Your email address — used to identify your account and sign you in.
  • Your username — the display name you choose.
  • Your flashcards and decks — everything you create or import.
  • Your review history — scheduling data (due dates, intervals, ease, pass/fail results) that makes spaced repetition work.
  • Session tokens — a random token stored on your device so you stay signed in.

If you sign in with Apple or Google, the app receives the email address and name that provider releases to it, and nothing else. If you use Apple's Hide My Email, only the relay address is stored.

Where it is stored

All data lives in a Cloudflare D1 database behind a Cloudflare Worker. Cloudflare acts as the infrastructure provider. Depending on where the database is provisioned and where you connect from, data may be stored or processed in the EU, and EU data-protection rules may apply to it.

What Flash does not do

  • No analytics or usage tracking of any kind.
  • No advertising, and no advertising identifiers.
  • No tracking across other apps or websites.
  • No selling or renting of data. Ever.
  • No sharing with third parties beyond the infrastructure needed to run the app (Cloudflare) and the sign-in providers you choose to use (Apple, Google) — and those are used for authentication only.

Deleting your account

The app has a Delete account button in the footer of the decks screen. Using it removes your account, your decks, your cards and your entire review history immediately. There is no soft-delete and no recovery — once it is gone, it is gone.

Children

Flash is a general-audience study app. It is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page changes with it.
